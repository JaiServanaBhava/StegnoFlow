🎯 Quick Viva Answers for Examination Judges

Q: Why does LSB modification not corrupt the image visually?
A: 8-bit color channels range from 0 to 255. Altering the 0th bit changes the intensity by at most ±1 part in 256 (~0.39%), which falls far below the Just Noticeable Difference (JND) threshold of human eye photoreceptors.


Q: How does Westfeld-Pfitzmann Chi-Square detection work?
A: In natural photos, the histogram of even/odd pairs (2k, 2k+1) is asymmetric. Sequential LSB steganography uniformly flips 0s and 1s, equalizing frequencies between 2k and 2k+1. The Chi-Square statistic measures the probability that observed counts match an equalized distribution. High probability (>65%) statistically proves stego presence.


Q: How does PRNG Scattering defeat Chi-Square attacks?
A: Traditional Chi-Square attacks evaluate sequential blocks from top-left. StegnoFlow uses a cryptographic keystream to scatter bit coordinates pseudorandomly across the entire image space, maintaining low local density and evading linear scanning detectors.
